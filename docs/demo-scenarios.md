# Demo scenarios (R11)

Three scenarios for the demo, each with a ready-to-use `/recommend` payload (contract C1) and
seed rows for Josh's farm/field/soil-test/fertilizer-log tables. The exact same data is in
[`docs/contract-fixtures/demo_scenarios.json`](contract-fixtures/demo_scenarios.json).

**Every number below is real, not hand-calculated** -- each `recommend_response` was produced
by actually running the real engine (`src/engine/recommendation_engine.py`'s `recommend()`,
`predict_mode=real`) against its paired `recommend_request`, using the real reference tables
(`ml/data/external/`) that are already in the repo. If a reference table changes (a new PAU
dose, a corrected price, a new soil-adjustment row), these numbers will need re-running, not
just re-reading.

All three fields are placed near Ludhiana, Punjab (30.90, 75.85) -- the same demo region
`ml/data/external/seasonal_weather.csv`'s `punjab_ludhiana` key already uses. Coordinates are
small offsets around that point, not any real farmer's actual plot.

**Ask Saloni to run all three through the live `/recommend` endpoint** (not just the direct
engine call used here) before the demo, and reconcile anything surprising -- in particular:
rice and maize both emit a `UserWarning` from `risk_analyzer`/`cost.py` ("no sourced season
length for crop_id=... falling back to credit_window_days") since only wheat and barley have
a real sourced season length in `growth_stages.csv` right now (see R8/R10's season-window
work) -- expected, not a bug, but worth knowing it'll show up in server logs for these two.

## 1. Wheat field with a history of over-application

**Story:** a farmer who's been applying urea generously gets told their nitrogen is already
well above what the crop needs, with a real cost number attached.

**Field:** `fld_demo_wheat_overuse`, 4.5 acres, 30.911, 75.847 (Ludhiana district), sown 1 Nov
2026, wheat, irrigated, generic variety. Soil test 20 Oct 2026: N 300 kg/ha (medium), P 15
kg/ha (medium), K 150 kg/ha (medium), pH 7.0, organic carbon 0.6%, moisture 35%. Fertilizer
log: 150 kg/acre urea applied 5 Nov 2026 (just after sowing).

**Recommendation date (demo "today"):** 25 Dec 2026, growth stage `second_irrigation` (~54
days after sowing).

**Verified outcome:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Risk level | **high** |
| Risk reason | "Applied N is 170.5 kg/ha, 335% of the 51.0 kg/ha the crop needs." |
| Recommended | urea, **22.4 kg/acre** (vs. 150 kg/acre previously applied) |
| Over-application reduction | **85.1%** |
| Cost | ₹132.71/acre now vs. ₹888.00/acre previously -- **saving ₹755.29/acre** |

Why the numbers land here: wheat's standard N dose is 123.6 kg/ha. The 150 kg/acre urea
already applied converts to ~170.5 kg/ha N, and at this crop's 42.6% nitrogen-use efficiency
(`nutrient_efficiency.csv`, sourced from Kaur et al. 2023) that credits 72.6 kg/ha against the
dose -- leaving only 51.0 kg/ha still needed, against 170.5 kg/ha already applied. P and K get
no such credit (nothing was applied for them), so P still shows its full standard dose in
`nutrient_balance` even though the *recommended product* is pure urea (P's need doesn't clear
the threshold to add DAP to the schedule at this stage -- see `nutrient_balance.p` in the
fixture for the full number).

## 2. Rice field with low soil nitrogen and heavy rain forecast

**Story:** a farmer with genuinely nitrogen-poor soil is heading into a top-dress window right
as heavy rain is forecast -- the schedule itself shows the delay, not just a risk warning.

**Field:** `fld_demo_rice_rainhold`, 3.0 acres, 30.889, 75.862 (Ludhiana district), nursery
sown 5 June 2026, rice, irrigated, generic variety, no fertilizer logged yet. Soil test 25 May
2026: N **150 kg/ha (low)**, P 15 kg/ha (medium), K 150 kg/ha (medium), pH 7.0, organic carbon
0.6%, moisture 45%.

**Recommendation date:** 25 July 2026, growth stage `active_tillering`, with **25mm of rain
forecast** (above `agronomy_rules.yaml`'s 20mm rain-hold threshold).

**Verified outcome:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Risk level | **medium** |
| Risk reason | "Soil N tests low, and only 0.0 kg/ha was applied against the 103.8 kg/ha needed (0%)." |
| Recommended | urea, 60.9 kg/acre total, split across two stages |
| **Schedule (the actual rain-hold effect)** | `active_tillering`: apply by **28 June** (delayed 2 days), note: *"Delayed 2 days for forecast rain"*. `panicle_initiation`: apply by **12 July** (also delayed 2 days), same note. |
| Cost / saving | ₹360.58/acre estimated; previous cost and saving are both `null` -- **no fertilizer log exists for this field, so there's no baseline to compare against.** Never invented, per the contract's own rule. |

This is the cleanest illustration of the rain-hold feature: both nitrogen split stages carry
an explicit, farmer-readable delay note and a pushed-back date, generated by
`npk_calculator.to_products()` from `agronomy_rules.yaml`'s `rain_hold_mm`/`rain_hold_days`.

## 3. Healthy maize field

**Story:** a field that's already being managed reasonably well gets a recommendation that
mostly reinforces standard practice, not a big correction -- and along the way, two real data
gaps surface honestly instead of silently.

**Field:** `fld_demo_maize_healthy`, 2.5 acres, 30.902, 75.831 (Ludhiana district), sown 15
June 2026, maize, irrigated, generic variety. Soil test 10 June 2026: N 300 kg/ha (medium), P
15 kg/ha (medium), K 150 kg/ha (medium), pH 7.0, organic carbon 0.6%, moisture 40%. Fertilizer
log: 100 kg/acre urea + 50 kg/acre DAP, both applied 16 June 2026 (basal, just after sowing).

**Recommendation date:** 1 July 2026, growth stage `sowing`.

**Verified outcome:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Risk level | **low** |
| Risk reason | "Applied and needed amounts are close across all nutrients, with no soil-health or runoff concerns flagged." |
| Recommended | DAP 52.2 kg/acre + MOP 20.0 kg/acre at sowing, urea split 18.1 + 19.3 kg/acre at knee-high/pre-tasseling |
| Cost / saving | ₹1,629.86/acre estimated vs. ₹1,942.00/acre previously -- **saving ₹312.14/acre** |

**Two honest data gaps surface here, in `explanation.data_notes`, not silently:**
1. *"No credit was given for last season's fertilizer because no use-efficiency figure is
   available for the P nutrient."* -- `nutrient_efficiency.csv` only has a sourced efficiency
   for nitrogen (42.6%, Kaur et al. 2023); P and K are `TODO(data)` for every crop, not just
   this scenario. The DAP already applied genuinely did supply phosphorus, but the engine
   can't credit it without a sourced efficiency number, so it correctly asks for the full
   standard P dose again rather than guessing.
2. *"mop's cost is unavailable (no verified price yet); it is not included in the cost
   breakdown."* -- MOP is still recommended and dosed (the field needs K2O), just excluded
   from `cost.breakdown`/`estimated_cost_inr_per_acre`, per the contract's missing-data policy
   Saloni implemented after the MOP price re-verification (see `PROGRESS.md`).

**Don't read "healthy" as "nothing recommended, nothing to see"** -- maize genuinely needs a
full P/K basal application regardless of what was applied last time (no P/K credit exists yet
for any crop), so the schedule has real substance. "Low risk" is the honest signal here, not
"small numbers."
